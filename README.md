## The one-liner
A Factorio-like browser game where **AI models are the machines** and
**tokens are the resource**. Jobs roll in on a belt; you wire models into
pipelines; profit = payouts − token spend − blown deadlines.

## Why it matters (Shrikanth's words)
- A great way for **AI companies to test candidates**: how well do you
  actually manage models, cost, and quality under pressure?
- A way for **beginners to learn working with AI**: which model for which
  job, what tokens cost, why routing matters.
- Fun first. The assessment/onboarding angles are the business case.
